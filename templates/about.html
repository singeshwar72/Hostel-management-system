Our Residential Ecosystem

BCE offers meticulously designed residential blocks that provide safety, comfort, and a thriving peer-to-peer learning environment.

The Heritage Blocks (1 & 2)

These classic 3-floor residential blocks feature a mix of single and triple occupancy rooms. Tailored primarily for senior engineering undergraduates, they offer quiet study zones, traditional campus charm, and close proximity to academic buildings.

The Modern Nexus (Hostel 3)

A newly developed, multi-story facility designed for high-capacity housing. Hostel 3 is fully integrated with our smart management system, featuring automated room occupancy tracking, high-speed Wi-Fi nodes, and expansive recreational lounges.

Smart Digital Living

Say goodbye to paper registers. Our centralized Hostel Management System (HMS) provides a direct support ticketing pipeline. Students can instantly request maintenance, manage mess dues, and secure digital gate passes directly from their smartphones.

Security & Wellness

Your safety is our priority. Our hostels are equipped with 24/7 CCTV surveillance, biometric campus entry, round-the-clock administrative wardens, and on-campus medical assistance to ensure absolute peace of mind.
